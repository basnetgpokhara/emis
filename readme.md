## About EMIS 
**EMIS School Management System**. Any School, Institution, Campus can manage student & staff detail with the different helpful module.
Fees, Payroll, Transaction, Library, Hostel, Attendance, Transport, Assignment, Download, SMS & Email Alert, Online Payment Gateway, User and Role manage with powerful ACL are the Key features of EMIS.
